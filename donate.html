Transparency

Where Your Money Goes

We believe in radical transparency. Here's how donations are allocated across AYAA's programs.

50%

Girls' Dormitory

The top priority right now. Half of all donations go directly to construction of the dormitory at Ayaa Senior Secondary School — the most urgent safety need for our female students.

30%

School Operations

Teacher salaries, school supplies, textbooks, maintenance, and student support — the day-to-day costs that keep 200+ students learning every single day.

20%

Water & Community

Supporting our WaterStep water partnership at the school and funding community support services for South Sudanese families in Louisville, Kentucky.

Double Your Impact

Does Your Employer Offer Donation Matching?

Thousands of companies — including many of the largest employers in Louisville — offer donation matching programs through platforms like Benevity, YourCause, and Bright Funds. If your employer participates, they will match your donation to AYAA dollar-for-dollar (or sometimes two-to-one or three-to-one).

This is one of the most powerful ways to amplify your generosity at no additional cost to you. A $100 donation becomes $200 or $300 — and for a small nonprofit like AYAA, that difference is enormous.

To check if your employer matches:

  1. Log in to your HR portal or ask your HR team
  2. Search for our organization name or EIN: 88-3720394
  3. Submit your matching gift request after making your donation
  4. AYAA will confirm your donation and process the match
Need Help? Contact Us.

Simon Ottaviano is happy to provide any documentation your employer needs to process a matching gift. Call (502) 235-3913 or email simonORealtor@gmail.com.
