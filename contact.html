Get Involved

There Are Many Ways to Help

Whether you have an hour or a year, a skill or just a heart — AYAA welcomes everyone who wants to be part of something real.

Volunteer in Louisville

Help with community events, fundraising, outreach, and services for South Sudanese families in the Louisville area. Volunteers of all backgrounds are welcome — especially those fluent in Acholi, Arabic, or Dinka.

Professional Skills Volunteering

Are you a grant writer, marketing professional, accountant, lawyer, teacher, or engineer? AYAA can use your expertise. We especially need help with grant applications to scale our programs.

Partner Organizations

Churches, mosques, schools, civic organizations, and businesses can partner with AYAA through joint fundraising, awareness events, or program sponsorship. Let's connect and explore what's possible.

Photography & Storytelling

The story of Ayaa school and the people of Magwi deserves to be told beautifully. If you're a photographer, videographer, or journalist who wants to help document and share this mission, we'd love to hear from you.

Education & Curriculum Support

Teachers and education professionals can support curriculum development, teacher training materials, and student assessment tools for Ayaa Senior Secondary School from anywhere in the world.

Be an Ambassador

Share AYAA's story with your networks — on social media, in your workplace, at your place of worship, in your community. Word-of-mouth from people who believe in this mission is the most powerful marketing we have.
